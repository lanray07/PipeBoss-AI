# PipeBoss AI Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 10, 2026

PipeBoss AI is an educational plumbing training simulation. The app is designed to be privacy-friendly and offline-first.

## Data Collection

PipeBoss AI does not collect, sell, share, or track personal data in the current release.

Game progress, coins, XP, unlocked jobs, tools, upgrades, settings, practice answers, review schedules, learning days, and usage counts are stored locally on your device. The app keeps up to 500 recent attempts. This data runs the simulation and shows your learning progress; it is not sent to us or to an analytics provider.

You may choose to share an aggregate learning report using the system share sheet. Reports contain topic accuracy and attempt totals, not your player name or payment details. Sharing sends the report only to the destinations you select. Reset Progress in Settings removes local training history; purchase access can be restored through Apple. A small on-device record of rating-request dates is retained to avoid repeated prompts after a reset.

## Purchases

Subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. PipeBoss AI does not receive or store your payment card details.

## Analytics and Advertising

The current release does not include third-party analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or cross-app tracking.

App Store metadata and public app copy may be translated during development using GitHub Actions. No player data, practice history, or Apple purchase data is sent to that translation service. Provider credentials are not included in the app.

## Educational Disclaimer

PipeBoss AI is for educational training and simulation only. Always follow local regulations and consult a qualified professional for real-world plumbing work.
