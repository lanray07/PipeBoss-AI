# PipeBoss AI Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 10, 2026

PipeBoss AI is an educational plumbing training simulation. The app is designed to be privacy-friendly and offline-first.

## Data Collection

PipeBoss AI does not collect, sell, share, or track personal data in the current release.

Game progress, coins, XP, unlocked jobs, tools, upgrades, and settings are stored locally on your device. This data is used only to run the training simulation.

## Purchases

Subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. PipeBoss AI does not receive or store your payment card details.

## Analytics and Advertising

The current release does not include third-party analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or cross-app tracking.

## Educational Disclaimer

PipeBoss AI is for educational training and simulation only. Always follow local regulations and consult a qualified professional for real-world plumbing work.
