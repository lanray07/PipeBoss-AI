# PipeBoss AI Support

PipeBoss AI is a plumbing training game for iPhone and iPad. It is for educational training and simulation only. Always follow local regulations and consult a qualified professional for real-world plumbing work.

## Get Help

Email **lanraybanks@gmail.com** with your app version, device model and a description of the issue. Please do not send passwords, payment card details or Apple account credentials. You can attach a screenshot after removing private information.

## Purchases and Restore

Open PipeBoss Store from Home or the Pro section. PipeBoss Pro Monthly and Yearly provide the same subscription access with different billing periods. One-time packs are also available from Business. Review included content before purchase: career level, tools and free-plan energy requirements still apply.

Use Restore Purchases in the store or Settings while signed into the Apple account that made the purchase. An internet connection is required for purchases and restore. If products cannot load, check your connection and try the store's retry button.

You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your device's Apple account subscription settings: https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions

## Offline Progress

Core training works offline and progress is stored on your device. Deleting the app or using Reset Progress can remove that progress. There is no cloud progress sync in the current release. Restoring purchases restores eligible Apple purchase access, not deleted learning history.

## Languages

English is the complete training language. The Spanish starter pilot is partial and falls back to English. Further translations will be released after language and plumbing-safety review.
